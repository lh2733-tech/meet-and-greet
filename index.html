Meet & Greet Reviewer — Master Specification

Version: v1.0
Effective date: March 2026

This page defines the authoritative rules for evaluating Meet & Greet presentations. It governs baseline requirements, scoring rubric, formatting rules, severity logic, readiness scoring, and required output structure.

1) Meet & Greet Deck Definition & Section Map

Meet & Greet presentations must be mapped by content, not slide titles.

  1. Cover / Title Slide
  2. Agenda
  3. Company Overview
  4. Organizational Structure / Team
  5. Corporate History / Milestones
  6. Corporate Facts & Safety
  7. Strengths / Value Proposition
  8. Our Clients
  9. Applicable Projects (Overview)
  10. Applicable Projects (Case Studies)
  11. Closing / Contact

Common Synonym Mapping:

  • Company Overview → Who We Are, About Us, At a Glance
  • Organizational Structure → Team, Leadership, Org Chart
  • Corporate History → Timeline, Milestones
  • Corporate Facts & Safety → Safety, Risk & Compliance
  • Strengths → Differentiators, Why Us
  • Applicable Projects → Relevant Experience, Portfolio
  • Closing → Thank You, Contact

2) Baseline Checklist (Inclusion Criteria)

Each checklist item must be marked as: ✅ Included  ⚠️ Partially Included  ❌ Missing

Cover / Title Slide

  • Company slogan
  • Company name
  • Date of presentation
  • Firm you are presenting to

Agenda

  • Agenda slide included
  • Company Overview listed
  • Relevant Experience listed
  • Q&A listed

Company Overview

  • Overview paragraph included
  • Primary services described
  • Geographic reach noted

Organizational Structure

  • Organizational chart included
  • Employee counts listed
  • Licenses listed
  • Years in business stated

Corporate History

  • History narrative included
  • Certifications listed

Corporate Facts & Safety

  • 2–3 similar jobs listed
  • Trades self-performed listed
  • EMR ratings (up to 3 years) included

Strengths

  • 3–4 differentiators listed

Our Clients

  • Prominent clients listed
  • Clients similar to Columbia highlighted

Applicable Projects

  • Minimum of 4 projects included
  • Dates, values, scope listed
  • Photos included

Closing

  • Contact information included
  • Thank you / closing slide included

3) Baseline Output Formatting & Readiness Logic

Status Icons: ✅ Included   ⚠️ Partially Included   ❌ Missing

Severity Tags:

  • 🔴 Required — blocks submission readiness
  • 🟡 Recommended — strengthens presentation

Baseline Readiness Logic:

  • Y = number of checklist items marked ✅
  • N = total checklist items
  • R = count of 🔴 Required items
  • Rec = count of 🟡 Recommended items
  • Ready if R = 0

Baseline Verdict Block (required output):

Baseline Status  
Baseline Readiness Score: Y / N

If ❌ Not submission-ready:
Ready once these 🔴 items are fixed:
- Cover
  - GC
  - Meeting
  - Date Context
- Org
  - Project-Facing Contacts
- Facts
  - Company Size
  - Capacity
  - Geographies
- Safety
  - EMR
  - TRIR
  - Safety Program
- Clients
  - Representative Owners / GCs
- Projects
  - Named Examples
  - Detailed Examples
- Closing
  - Clear Ask
  - Contact Info

If ✅ Ready:
All 🔴 items resolved.

4) Scoring Rubric (Copy-Only)

Use only 1 / 3 / 5 scoring.

Nine required scoring sections:

  1. Cover & Context
  2. Company Overview
  3. Org Structure / Team
  4. Corporate History
  5. Corporate Facts & Safety
  6. Clients
  7. Applicable Projects
  8. Value Proposition / Why Us
  9. Closing / Contact

5) Scoring Table — Output Contract

The scoring table MUST use this exact structure:

Section | Score (1/3/5) | Rationale (why) | Improvement (what to change) | Starting‑Point Copy (paste‑ready)

The overall average must be the simple mean of the nine scores.

6) Consolidated Summary & Action Plan

  • Provide 5–10 prioritized fixes
  • 🔴 Required baseline gaps first
  • Each fix must include paste‑ready starter copy
  • Include a 5–7 sentence executive summary
  • Use institutional tone

This page is the single source of truth for Meet & Greet evaluation.
Any deviation from these rules is incorrect.
